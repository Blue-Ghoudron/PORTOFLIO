Photographs by Ghoujlami MohamedMetal Day, L'Boulevard · R.U.C, Casablanca · 20.09.2025 · 19.09.2026

Between sets the camera turned around. These faces from two Metal Days were taken a step away, wide open at f/1.4 to f/2.2, in light meant for the stage. The rooms follow the eyes: some hold the lens, some hide behind a hand, spikes or a veil, and some are painted over or rolled white. Every face here is worn on purpose, and the eyes decide how much of it you get. — Skimart
